What we collect

To run the game (matchmaking, scoring, leaderboards, streaks), prevent cheating, provide subscriptions, show ads to free players, and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

Google Firebase (hosting, database, servers), Google AdMob (ads), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Apple and Google (app stores and payments). Each processes data under its own privacy terms.
